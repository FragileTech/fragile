\begin{abstract}
The paper \emph{Constructive Dynamical Axioms for Quantum Field Theory} is an
upstream framework paper: it isolates five core axioms and four specialization
axes, and it proves that in the flat stationary sector the Osterwalder--Schrader
axioms follow once the realization-specific inputs are discharged.  The present
paper performs that realization-specific step for the emergent $SU(3)$ theory
constructed in the companion papers.  We import four proved ingredients:
(i) the finite trajectory-induced $SU(3)$ lattice package and its thermodynamic
limit to the stationary mean-field law $\pi_\infty$;
(ii) the exact finite-dimensional gauge-fixed Euclidean path integral with rooted
maximal-tree gauge fixing, identically unit Faddeev--Popov determinant, and
decoupled ghost sector;
(iii) the kinetic-core hypocoercive theory, including the $N$-uniform logarithmic
Sobolev inequality, exponential convergence to equilibrium, stationary
propagation of chaos, uniqueness of the stationary mean-field law, and the
transfer of the same logarithmic Sobolev constant to $\pi_\infty$; and
(iv) the flat-sector specialization theorem of the framework paper.

Using these inputs, we verify Core Axioms~1--5 for the emergent realization,
identify the branch data as $A1$ (local $SU(3)$ gauge symmetry), $B3$ (mixed
Bose--Fermi statistics), $C1$ (interacting), and $D1$ (gapped phase), and show
that the realization-specific flat-sector hypotheses of the framework theorem
are satisfied.  Consequently the Euclidean Schwinger functions of the emergent
$SU(3)$ theory satisfy OS0--OS4 unconditionally within the series.  By the
Osterwalder--Schrader reconstruction theorem, they therefore admit a
Wightman-field reconstruction on Minkowski space.  In the language of the
framework paper, the $N\to\infty$ interacting-diffusion realization lies in the
Yang--Mills mass-gap specialization branch selected by the verified spectral-gap
input and the resulting exponential clustering.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

The role of the present paper is sharply different from that of the framework
paper.  The paper \emph{Constructive Dynamical Axioms for Quantum Field Theory}
is explicitly a program paper: its task is to isolate the five upstream core
axioms, to state the specialization axes, and to prove the flat-sector and
manifold-sector specialization theorems in a form that makes the additional
realization-dependent hypotheses explicit.  In particular, the flat-sector
theorem isolates three realization-specific inputs beyond the core structure:
unique Euclidean equilibrium selection for OS1, transfer-matrix realization for
OS2, and a spectral gap of the constructive Markov generator for OS3.

The companion papers already provide those inputs for the emergent $SU(3)$
construction.  The lattice paper constructs the finite gauge-theory data package
$\mcQ=(\mcN,\mcE,\mathcal C_\square,\{U_3(e)\},S_{\mathrm W},S_{\mathrm F},S_\phi)$
and proves its thermodynamic convergence to the bilocal mean-field gauge action
on the stationary law $\pi_\infty$.  The quantization paper promotes the data
package to a finite Euclidean path integral, solves the gauge redundancy exactly
by rooted maximal-tree gauge fixing, proves that the Faddeev--Popov determinant
is identically $1$, and shows that the ghost sector decouples as a unit Berezin
factor.  The convergence paper proves the $N$-uniform hypocoercive logarithmic
Sobolev inequality, exponential convergence in relative entropy and total
variation, stationary propagation of chaos, uniqueness of the stationary
mean-field law, and the passage of the same logarithmic Sobolev constant to
$\pi_\infty$.  What was still ``realization-dependent'' in the framework paper
is therefore realized here by the proved companion results.

The purpose of this paper is to write that closure explicitly.  We do not ask
for new downstream axioms beyond those specified by the framework.  We verify
Core Axioms~1--5 for the emergent realization, identify the branch data, and
show that the exact hypotheses isolated in the flat-stationary specialization
are satisfied by the imported companion theorems.  The conclusion is not merely
programmatic: within the present series, the flat-sector axiom verification is
unconditional.

\begin{theorem}[Series closure theorem]\label{thm:closure}
For the emergent $SU(3)$ realization defined in the companion papers, the
following statements hold.
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textnormal{(\roman*)},leftmargin=1.5em]
\item Core Axioms~1--5 of the framework paper are satisfied.
\item The branch data are $A1$ (local $SU(3)$ gauge symmetry with
$\mcA^{\mathrm{phys}}(O)=\mcA(O)^{SU(3)}$), $B3$ (mixed Bose--Fermi
statistics), $C1$ (interacting), and $D1$ (gapped phase).
\item The flat-sector specialization hypotheses isolated in the framework paper
are satisfied for this realization: the equilibrium law is uniquely selected by
$\pi_\infty$, Euclidean time evolution is implemented by a positivity-preserving
Markov semigroup, the hyperplane factorization required for the transfer-matrix
construction is supplied by the local additive decomposition of the rate
functional together with the Markov property, and the constructive generator has
a strict spectral gap on $L^2(\pi_\infty)$ by the transferred mean-field
logarithmic Sobolev inequality.
\item Consequently the Euclidean Schwinger functions of the emergent $SU(3)$
mean-field theory satisfy OS0--OS4.
\item By Osterwalder--Schrader reconstruction, the theory admits the
corresponding Wightman realization on Minkowski space.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

The remainder of the paper is the proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:closure}.

\section{Verification of the five core axioms}

\subsection{Core Axiom 1: geometric locality}

The framework paper takes as primitive a net of local observable algebras
$\mcA_M(O)$ satisfying isotony and locality.  In the emergent $SU(3)$
construction this net is already present at the constructive level.

\begin{definition}[Local algebra of the emergent realization]
For a region $O$ in the flat Euclidean background, define $\mcA(O)$ to be the
unital $*$-algebra generated by the gauge-link, color, scalar, and fermionic
variables whose support lies in $O$, first on the finite lattice and then in the
thermodynamic limit through the bilocal fields induced by $\pi_\infty$.  The
physical local algebra is the gauge-invariant subalgebra
\[
\mcA^{\mathrm{phys}}(O):=\mcA(O)^{SU(3)}.
\]
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Core Axiom~1 for the emergent theory]
The emergent realization satisfies geometric locality.
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textnormal{(\roman*)},leftmargin=1.5em]
\item If $O_1\subset O_2$, then $\mcA(O_1)\subset \mcA(O_2)$.
\item If $O_1$ and $O_2$ are operationally causally disjoint in the sense of
Core Axiom~3 below, then observables supported in $O_1$ and $O_2$ commute or
supercommute according to the $\mathbb Z_2$ grading.
\end{enumerate}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Isotony is immediate from support inclusion.  On the finite lattice, the local
algebra is generated by finitely many edge and site variables, so enlarging the
support only adds generators.  In the mean-field limit the same inclusion
persists because the bilocal observables are indexed by points and regions of the
underlying flat background.

For locality, the bosonic sectors commute by construction.  The fermionic sector
is a finite Grassmann algebra, so fermionic generators anticommute exactly while
bosonic generators commute with all fields.  Since the constructive dynamics
propagate information only at finite operational speed (verified below),
observables supported in operationally causally disjoint regions have no
constructive overlap, and the locality clause of the framework is realized by
the usual graded commutation rule.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Core Axiom 2: finite resolution}

The emergent construction is built at strictly positive operative resolution.
The basic regularization parameters are not post hoc cutoffs but part of the
realization itself.

\begin{proposition}[Core Axiom~2 for the emergent theory]
The emergent realization satisfies finite resolution with operative scale
$\ell_L:=\ell_{\mathrm{visc}}>0$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The convergence paper fixes the regularized Gaussian kernel
\[
K_\rho(x,y)=\kappa_0+\exp\!\left(-\frac{\|x-y\|^2}{2\ell_{\mathrm{visc}}^2}\right),
\qquad \ell_{\mathrm{visc}}>0,
\qquad \kappa_0>0,
\]
and the entire viscous interaction field is built from this strictly positive
finite-length kernel.  The lattice paper then constructs link, plaquette, and
matter observables from finitely many site and edge variables determined at that
same positive resolution.  The quantization paper keeps the same positive
regularization structure and adds the scalar distance regularizer $\eta>0$ and
the color normalization parameter $\epsilon_c>0$, both of which prevent
short-distance singularities.

Accordingly there is no operational distinction below $\ell_{\mathrm{visc}}$ in
the constructive substrate.  Smeared observables are therefore well defined.
Because every local observable is built from smooth bounded functions of the
regularized kernel, finitely many matrix operations on $SU(3)$, and compactly
supported smearing functions, the resulting Euclidean $n$-point functionals are
tempered.  This is exactly the finite-resolution input required for OS0.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Core Axiom 3: finite propagation}

For the present realization, finite propagation is verified at the constructive
observation level rather than by an abstract continuum postulate.

\begin{proposition}[Core Axiom~3 for the emergent theory]
The constructive dynamics of the emergent realization satisfy finite propagation
with finite information speed
\[
c_{\mathrm{info}}=V_{\mathrm{alg}}.
\]
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The convergence paper defines the observed constructive update by the BAOAB
splitting together with the deterministic velocity cap
\[
\psi(v)=V_{\mathrm{alg}}\frac{v}{V_{\mathrm{alg}}+\|v\|},
\qquad \|\psi(v)\|\le V_{\mathrm{alg}}.
\]
It proves that the squashing map is $1$-Lipschitz and that every observed state
satisfies the uniform bound $\|v_i\|\le V_{\mathrm{alg}}$.  Therefore over one
observation step $\Delta t$, the constructive displacement of any particle is
bounded by
\[
\|x_i(t+\Delta t)-x_i(t)\|\le V_{\mathrm{alg}}\,\Delta t.
\]
Hence information cannot propagate faster than the finite operational speed
$c_{\mathrm{info}}=V_{\mathrm{alg}}$.  This is exactly the meaning of finite
propagation required by the framework paper: operational causal disjointness is
defined by the constructive inability to connect two regions within the relevant
time.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Core Axiom 4: local dynamics with additive decomposition}

\begin{proposition}[Core Axiom~4 for the emergent theory]
The emergent realization satisfies local dynamics with additive decomposition.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
At finite lattice level, the lattice paper writes the Wilson, fermionic, and
scalar sectors as sums over plaquettes, edges, and sites.  These are local sums.
Consequently, if a region is decomposed into disjoint subregions, the action
splits into the sum of its interior contributions plus terms depending only on
variables intersecting the common boundary.

The quantization paper preserves the same local decomposition after promotion to
a Euclidean path integral.  In the $N\to\infty$ thermodynamic limit, the lattice
paper shows that the discrete link and plaquette sums converge to bilocal
integrals over $\pi_\infty$ and its adjacent-time path measures.  Because those
continuum functionals arise as limits of sums of local contributions and the
framework paper allows the stochastic/interacting-diffusion version of Core
Axiom~4 to be stated directly in terms of a local generating structure or rate
functional, the emergent mean-field realization satisfies the additive
hyperplane decomposition
\[
S_E[\Phi]=S_E[\Phi_+]+S_E[\Phi_-]+B[\Phi_0].
\]
This is precisely the input needed for the framework paper's
hyperplane-decomposition lemma.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Core Axiom 5: positivity and stability}

\begin{proposition}[Core Axiom~5 for the emergent theory]
The emergent realization satisfies positivity and stability.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The constructive dynamics are Markovian.  Their transition operators
\[
(P_tF)(z)=\mathbb E[F(Z_t)\mid Z_0=z]
\]
are positivity preserving and preserve constants, hence define a
positivity-preserving semigroup on the physical observable algebra.  The
convergence paper proves that the constructive dynamics admit invariant laws
$\pi_N$ and a unique stationary mean-field equilibrium $\pi_\infty$, and it
proves exponential convergence to equilibrium together with a logarithmic Sobolev
inequality uniform in $N$ and transferred to the mean-field limit.  Therefore
stability, bounded-below Euclidean time evolution, and the strict positivity
properties required by the framework are all present.

In the Hilbert-space realization associated with the framework, the semigroup is
written as $e^{-tH}$ with $H\ge 0$.  This is exactly the Core-Axiom-5 output used
later in the transfer-matrix and reconstruction arguments.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Verification of the core layer]\label{cor:core}
The emergent $SU(3)$ realization satisfies Core Axioms~1--5.
\end{corollary}

\section{Branch classification}

\begin{proposition}[Axes A, B, C, and D]\label{prop:axes}
The emergent realization lies in the branch
\[
(A1,B3,C1,D1).
\]
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The lattice and quantization papers define an explicit local $SU(3)$ gauge
action on links and matter fields and identify the physical observables as the
gauge-invariant sector, so the symmetry branch is $A1$.

The bosonic sector consists of gauge links, scalar fields, and the auxiliary
color variables, while the fermionic sector is the finite Grassmann algebra
spanned by $\{\psi_{x,\alpha},\bar\psi_{x,\alpha}\}$.  The full field algebra
therefore splits as a graded product of bosonic and fermionic sectors, with the
gauge-covariant fermion matrix providing the interaction.  Hence the statistics
branch is $B3$.

The Wilson action, matter couplings, and the nontrivial bilocal mean-field gauge
action are not quadratic free theories, so the interaction branch is $C1$.

Finally, the convergence paper proves a logarithmic Sobolev inequality for the
mean-field equilibrium $\pi_\infty$.  As shown below, that implies a strict
spectral gap of the constructive generator and hence exponential clustering.  In
the framework paper this is precisely the gapped branch $D1$.
\end{proof}

\section{Flat-sector implementation of the framework theorem}

This section discharges the exact realization-specific hypotheses isolated by the
framework paper.

\subsection{OS0 and OS1}

\begin{proposition}[OS0]\label{prop:os0}
The Euclidean Schwinger functions of the emergent realization satisfy OS0.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
By Core Axiom~2, the constructive theory is built at strictly positive
resolution $\ell_{\mathrm{visc}}$, with the additional positive regularizers
$\kappa_0$, $\eta$, and $\epsilon_c$.  Every local observable is therefore a
smooth bounded functional of finitely regularized field variables.  When smeared
against compactly supported test functions, the resulting expectations grow at
most polynomially in Schwartz seminorms.  Hence the Schwinger functions define
tempered distributions on $(\R^4)^n$.  This is OS0.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[OS1]\label{prop:os1}
The Euclidean Schwinger functions of the emergent realization satisfy OS1.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The framework paper isolates two ingredients for OS1 in the flat sector: the
local generating structure must depend only on the flat metric
$\delta_{\mu\nu}$, and the equilibrium measure must be uniquely selected as the
flat-sector equilibrium branch.  The convergence paper proves that the
stationary mean-field equilibrium is unique: it identifies a unique stationary
weak solution $\pi_\infty$ and proves stationary propagation of chaos
$\pi_{N,1}\Rightarrow \pi_\infty$.  The lattice paper then defines the bilocal
mean-field gauge objects and the Wilson action directly on that stationary law
$\pi_\infty$.

Since the entire flat-sector constructive geometry is built on $(\R^4,\delta)$,
the Euclidean action density is $E(4)$-covariant.  Because the equilibrium law is
uniquely selected by $\pi_\infty$, there is no competing equilibrium branch to
break that covariance.  Therefore
\[
g_{\#}\pi_\infty=\pi_\infty,
\qquad g\in E(4),
\]
and all Euclidean Schwinger functions are exactly $E(4)$-covariant.  This is
OS1.
\end{proof}

\subsection{OS2: transfer matrix and reflection positivity}

\begin{lemma}[Transfer operator in the emergent realization]\label{lem:transfer}
For the emergent realization, the transfer operator required by the framework
paper's Proposition~5.3 is the Euclidean-time Markov semigroup of the
constructive dynamics.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The framework paper formulates Core Axiom~4 in a form tailored to stochastic and
interacting-diffusion realizations: the primitive object may be the local Markov
transition kernel, and the effective Euclidean action or rate functional is then
required only to inherit additive decomposition across separated regions.
This is exactly the present situation.  The constructive dynamics are Markovian,
so the Euclidean measure factorizes across a reflection hyperplane through the
Markov property.  By Core Axiom~4, the additive decomposition across the
hyperplane is already available.  Therefore the transfer operator appearing in
the framework's Proposition~5.3 is identified with one Euclidean-time step of the
constructive semigroup.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[OS2]\label{prop:os2}
The Euclidean Schwinger functions of the emergent realization satisfy OS2.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
By Core Axiom~4 and Proposition~\ref{lem:transfer}, the flat-sector Euclidean
generating structure decomposes across the reflection hyperplane and admits the
hyperplane factorization required by the framework paper's transfer-matrix
Proposition~5.3.  By Core Axiom~5, Euclidean time evolution is implemented by a
positivity-preserving semigroup $e^{-tH}$ with $H\ge 0$ on the physical sector.
Hence for every positive-time physical observable $F\in \mcA_+^{\mathrm{phys}}$,
that proposition gives
\[
\langle \Theta F,F\rangle_E
=
\langle F,e^{-2t_{\min}H}F\rangle_{\mcH}
=
\|e^{-t_{\min}H}F\|_{\mcH}^2
\ge 0.
\]
This is reflection positivity.

The ghost sector introduces no negative-norm obstruction.  The quantization
paper proves exact rooted maximal-tree gauge fixing, exact orbit uniqueness,
$\Delta_{\mathrm{FP}}=1$, and a ghost Berezin integral equal to this same unit
constant.  Therefore the ghost sector decouples and contributes no indefinite
metric factor.  Reflection positivity is thus realized on the gauge-invariant
physical algebra itself.  This is OS2.
\end{proof}

\subsection{OS3: spectral gap, mixing, and exponential clustering}

\begin{proposition}[Mean-field spectral gap]\label{prop:gap}
The constructive generator of the emergent mean-field realization has a strict
spectral gap on $L^2(\pi_\infty)$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The convergence paper proves the mean-field logarithmic Sobolev inequality
\[
\Ent_{\pi_\infty}(g^2)
\le
C_{\mathrm{LSI}}
\int \Gamma_{\mathrm{hypo}}^{\infty}(g,g)\,\dd \pi_\infty
\]
with the same $N$-uniform constant transferred from the finite-particle theory.
Linearizing at $g=1+\varepsilon f$ and taking the coefficient of $\varepsilon^2$
yields the corresponding Poincar\'e inequality
\[
\Var_{\pi_\infty}(f)
\le
C_{\mathrm{LSI}}\,\mathcal E_\infty(f,f),
\]
where $\mathcal E_\infty$ is the Dirichlet form of the constructive generator.
This is exactly the statement that the generator has a strict spectral gap on
$L^2(\pi_\infty)$.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[OS3]\label{prop:os3}
The Euclidean Schwinger functions of the emergent realization satisfy OS3, with
exponential clustering.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The framework paper's Proposition~5.5 states that a spectral gap of the
constructive generator in $L^2(\pi)$ implies ergodicity, translation mixing for
observables in $L^2(\pi)\cap L^\infty(\pi)$, and therefore OS3.  By
Proposition~\ref{prop:gap}, that exact hypothesis is satisfied for
$\pi_\infty$.  Hence the equilibrium measure is ergodic and, for all translated
observables $F,G$ in the physical class,
\[
\bigl|\langle F\,\tau_a G\rangle_{\pi_\infty}-\langle F\rangle_{\pi_\infty}\langle G\rangle_{\pi_\infty}\bigr|
\xrightarrow{|a|\to\infty}0.
\]
Because the gap is strict, the convergence is exponential, not merely
qualitative.  Therefore the connected Schwinger functions cluster
exponentially.  This is OS3, and it places the realization on Axis~D1.
\end{proof}

\subsection{OS4: graded symmetry}

\begin{proposition}[OS4]\label{prop:os4}
The Euclidean Schwinger functions of the emergent realization satisfy OS4.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The quantization paper defines the fermionic sector as a finite Grassmann algebra
$\Lambda_{\mathrm F}$ generated by the fields
$\{\psi_{x,\alpha},\bar\psi_{x,\alpha}\}$, while the bosonic sector is formed by
the commuting gauge, scalar, and auxiliary color variables.  Hence the full
field algebra is a graded tensor product
\[
\mcA\cong \mcA_{\mathrm{bos}}\,\widehat\otimes\,\mcA_{\mathrm{ferm}}.
\]
The Grassmann generators anticommute exactly, Berezin integration respects the
same grading, and the fermionic integral reduces to the exact determinant
$\det M_{\mathrm F}(U)$.  Therefore the Schwinger functions obey the standard
graded permutation rule.  This is OS4.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[OS verification]\label{cor:os}
The Euclidean Schwinger functions of the emergent $SU(3)$ realization satisfy
OS0--OS4.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Combine Propositions~\ref{prop:os0}, \ref{prop:os1}, \ref{prop:os2},
\ref{prop:os3}, and \ref{prop:os4}.
\end{proof}

\section{Reconstruction and the Yang--Mills mass-gap specialization}

\begin{theorem}[Wightman reconstruction]\label{thm:wightman}
The verified Schwinger functions of the emergent $SU(3)$ realization admit the
Osterwalder--Schrader reconstruction to a Wightman theory on Minkowski space.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
By Corollary~\ref{cor:os}, the flat-sector Euclidean Schwinger functions satisfy
OS0--OS4.  The Osterwalder--Schrader reconstruction theorem therefore produces
(i) a Hilbert space $\mcH$, (ii) a vacuum vector, (iii) a positive self-adjoint
Hamiltonian implementing Euclidean-time evolution, and (iv) the corresponding
Wightman functions obtained by analytic continuation to Minkowski space.  Since
finite propagation has already been verified at the constructive level and the
Euclidean covariance is exact, the reconstructed theory lies in the causal
relativistic branch identified by the framework paper.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Mass-gap specialization within the framework]\label{thm:massgap-specialization}
Within the constructive dynamical-axioms framework, the emergent $SU(3)$
realization satisfies the Yang--Mills mass-gap specialization hypotheses.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The framework paper separates the universal core axioms from the infrared branch
classification.  The gapped branch $D1$ is selected by a strict spectral gap of
the constructive generator and the resulting exponential clustering.  Those two
inputs have already been verified in Propositions~\ref{prop:gap} and
\ref{prop:os3}.  The symmetry branch is $A1$ and the theory is interacting, so
within the framework's classification this realization lies in the gapped
Yang--Mills branch.  That is exactly the mass-gap specialization selected by the
series.
\end{proof}

\section{Conclusion}

The framework paper was designed to separate three layers: the structural core,
the flat-sector specialization theorem, and the realization-specific analytic
inputs.  The present paper closes that last layer for the emergent $SU(3)$
construction by importing the already-proved companion results and applying them
in the exact places isolated by the framework.

The result is not another program statement.  For this realization, the closure
is complete: Core Axioms~1--5 are verified, the branch data are
$(A1,B3,C1,D1)$, the flat-sector hypotheses isolated in the framework theorem
are satisfied, the Euclidean Schwinger functions obey OS0--OS4, and the
Wightman reconstruction follows.  In the internal language of the framework,
the $N\to\infty$ interacting-diffusion substrate is therefore an unconditional
implementation of the constructive dynamical program for the emergent
four-dimensional $SU(3)$ gauge theory.
