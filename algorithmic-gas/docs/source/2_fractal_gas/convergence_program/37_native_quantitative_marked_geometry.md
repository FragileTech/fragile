# Quantitative native preparation, dense fields and marked spatial records

(sec-nqg-register)=
## 1. The proved phase and its exact preparation coordinates

:::{prf:definition} Quantitative marked-record register
:label: def-nqg-register

Retain every algorithm, landscape, donor, normalization, fitness,
component-Haar, collision, jitter, kinetic, boundary, arithmetic,
observation and physical-calibration parameter of
{prf:ref}`def-nqc-register` and {prf:ref}`def-nmg-complete-register`.
The positive stationary conclusions use their existing quadratic
count gas, its derived contraction and concentration tests, and the
actual incoming full marked QSD $\nu_N$. In particular
$0<V\le\min\{V_0,1\}$, $\nu\le\nu_g$, $r<1$, $q,s>0$ and
$0\le t\nu\le1/2$. Every consumed parameter is fixed as $N$ varies.
The population phase is the proved $\mu_*$.

Use $a_p,C_J,R_N,\delta_N,D_J$ of (NQC.1), (NQC.4), (NQC.14),
with proof moment order $p\ge2$ and all their primitive coefficients.
Here $\delta_N=e^{-c_*a_0N}$ is the actual low-alive exception,
and $R_N$ bounds $E_{\nu_N}W_\omega(L_N,\mu_*)$.
The compact pre-jitter record is $W=(Y,I,w)$, $w=v^c$,
with $|Y|\le R_D$, $I\in\{0,1\}$, $|w|\le V_c=k_cV$.
Write $\Theta_N=N^{-1}\sum_i\delta_{W_i}$ and
$\Theta_*=\Pi(\mu_*)$.

The original first-kick record uses

$$
X^J=Y+\sigma_JIZ^J,\qquad
U=w+t\nu\int K_\rho(X^J-X^{J\prime})(w'-w)\,d\lambda_J,
$$
$$
v_1=U-t\lambda X^J,\quad p_1=\alpha X^J+tU,\quad
m=a_xX^J+bU,\qquad \alpha=1-t^2\lambda .
\tag{NQG.1}
$$

For the actual finite swarm replace the integral by its literal
count average. Its self contribution is zero. Retain
$A=(p_1,v_1,m,I)$ and its exact constraint $m=p_1+tcv_1$.
Denote its actual empirical law by $\eta_N$, and its population
law from $\Theta_*$ by $\eta_*$. Source, generation, gate, fitness
and component metadata remain attached to the finite record.
The following transport concerns their physical coordinate projection;
it does not assign a metric or a tight limit to absolute slot labels.
Put

$$
d_A(A,A')^2=|p_1-p_1'|^2+|v_1-v_1'|^2
                         +|m-m'|^2+|I-I'|^2.
\tag{NQG.2}
$$

The redundant $m$ is retained in this metric and every posterior
formula. Its constraint does not increase the sampling dimension.
:::

(sec-nqg-compact)=
## 2. Compact preparation transport with the actual alive exception

:::{prf:lemma} Primitive stationary pre-jitter transport
:label: lem-nqg-compact-preparation

Use the actual coefficients of (PC.4)--(PC.8) and set

$$
\begin{gathered}
L_{\Pi x}=L_D^{\rm pos}[1+2/(\kappa_Cm_0)]
                      +(2R_D+1)R_x+L_{Vx},\\
L_{\Pi v}=(2R_D+1)R_v+L_{Vv},\qquad
L_\Pi=\max\{L_{\Pi x},L_{\Pi v}/\omega\},\\
B_{{\rm prep},N}=C_JN^{-a_p}+L_\Pi R_N+D_J\delta_N,\qquad
C_{\rm comp}=\max\{2R_D,2V_c,1\}.
\end{gathered}
\tag{NQG.3}
$$

For all admitted sufficiently large $N$,

$$
E_{\nu_N}W_{d_J}(\Theta_N,\Theta_*)\le B_{{\rm prep},N},\qquad
E_{\nu_N}W_{2,{\rm comp}}(\Theta_N,\Theta_*)^2
                       \le C_{\rm comp}B_{{\rm prep},N}.
\tag{NQG.4}
$$

The squared compact metric is
$|Y-Y'|^2+|w-w'|^2+|I-I'|^2$.
:::

:::{prf:proof}
The complete coupling of
{prf:ref}`lem-native-phase-preparation-coupling`, stopped before
jitter, bounds matched source discrepancies by
$L_D^{\rm pos}[1+2/(\kappa_Cm_0)]\delta_x$.
An unmatched outcome costs $2R_D$ in its source and one in its
jitter indicator, with expected fraction at most
$R_x\delta_x+R_v\delta_v$. Full component collisions contribute
$L_{Vx}\delta_x+L_{Vv}\delta_v$. Thus
$W_{d_J}(\Pi\mu,\Pi\mu')\le L_\Pi W_\omega(\mu,\mu')$
when both actual input alive fractions are at least $m_0$.
The original shared component Haar variables remain in this coupling.

On $H_N$, combine (NQC.5) with this inequality and $\mu'=\mu_*$.
On $H_N^c$ use diameter $D_J$, and retain its proved QSD probability
$\delta_N$. The stationary bound $R_N$ gives the first inequality.
Every compact pair satisfies

$$
|Y-Y'|^2+|w-w'|^2+|I-I'|^2
\le C_{\rm comp}d_J(W,W').
$$

Integrating under a $d_J$-optimal coupling proves the second.
No deterministic alive-floor premise replaces $H_N^c$.
:::

(sec-nqg-jitter)=
## 3. Original jitter and the correlated first kick

:::{prf:definition} Quadratic preparation transport coefficients
:label: def-nqg-quadratic-budget

Let $g_{d,P}=(E|Z_d|^P)^{1/P}$, $\ell_\rho=e^{-1/2}/\rho$,
and $\theta=4t\nu V_c\ell_\rho$. Define

$$
\begin{gathered}
C_X=\max\{4R_D,2\sigma_J^2d\},\qquad
C_U=(1+t\nu)\sqrt{2V_c}+\theta\sqrt{C_X},\\
C_\Gamma^2=(|\alpha|\sqrt{C_X}+tC_U)^2
 +(C_U+t\lambda\sqrt{C_X})^2
 +(|a_x|\sqrt{C_X}+bC_U)^2+1,\\
L_A^2=1+\alpha^2+t^2\lambda^2+a_x^2+t^2+1+b^2 .
\end{gathered}
\tag{NQG.5}
$$

For a proof order $P>2$, put

$$
\begin{gathered}
H_{J,P}=(R_D+\sigma_Jg_{d,P})^P,\quad
T_P=(1+V_c^2)H_{J,P},\\
J_P=2[(1+4\sqrt d)(1+4\sqrt dV_c)]^d+2,\quad
D_P^2=5+4V_c^2,\\
b_P=\left[2+d+\frac{d+4}{P-2}\right]^{-1},\quad
C_{{\rm sam},P}=6+6T_P+\frac32D_P^2\sqrt{J_P}.
\end{gathered}
\tag{NQG.6}
$$

Choose $P\ge\max\{p,3+4/d\}$, so $b_P\ge(2+2d)^{-1}>a_p$.
All these moments come from the original Gaussian jitter.
:::

:::{prf:lemma} Population first-record continuity from the compact source law
:label: lem-nqg-population-preparation

For any compact preparation probabilities $\Theta,\Theta'$, let
$\Gamma\Theta,\Gamma\Theta'$ be their population jitter/B1
coordinate laws (NQG.1). Then

$$
W_{2,A}(\Gamma\Theta,\Gamma\Theta')^2
\le C_\Gamma^2 W_{d_J}(\Theta,\Theta').
\tag{NQG.7}
$$
:::

:::{prf:proof}
Couple the compact sources at expected $d_J$ cost $\epsilon$ and
use the same original $Z^J$. Their squared discrepancies satisfy

$$
E|X^J-X^{J\prime}|^2
\le4R_DE|Y-Y'|+2\sigma_J^2dE|I-I'|\le C_X\epsilon,
\quad E|w-w'|^2\le2V_c\epsilon .
$$

Use an independent copy of the paired source law for the count
integral. Own and source velocity derivatives cost $1+t\nu$
in $L^2$, and own and source kernel derivatives cost
$4t\nu V_c\ell_\rho=\theta$ in $L^2$. One may equivalently
interpolate the sources, differentiate the literal integral, and
apply Minkowski to its source expectation. Hence

$$
\|U-U'\|_2\le(1+t\nu)\|w-w'\|_2+\theta\|X^J-X^{J\prime}\|_2
\le C_U\sqrt\epsilon .
$$

The count convexity gives $|U|\le V_c$ throughout.
The three linear coordinates in (NQG.1) have respectively the first
three costs in $C_\Gamma^2$; the indicator costs at most $\epsilon$.
Add the squares and minimize over source couplings.
This uses no independence between $U$ and its own jitter.
:::

:::{prf:lemma} Conditional independent-reference quadratic sampling
:label: lem-nqg-reference-sampling

Freeze the actual complete compact preparation array and its
component record. Evaluate each original independent jitter's
first kick against the deterministic jitter-mixture
$\lambda_J(\Theta_N)$, obtaining $U_i^0$ and empirical law
$\eta_N^0$ of $(p_i^0,v_{1i}^0,m_i^0,I_i)$.
These reference rows are conditionally independent, with mixture
law $\Gamma\Theta_N$, and

$$
E[W_{2,A}(\eta_N^0,\Gamma\Theta_N)^2\mid\text{compact record}]
\le L_A^2C_{{\rm sam},P}N^{-b_P}.
\tag{NQG.8}
$$

This does not assert independence of the actual first-kick rows.
:::

:::{prf:proof}
Parameterize the reference by $(X^J,U^0,I)$, where $|U^0|\le V_c$
and each row has $E|X^J|^P\le H_{J,P}$. The linear map to $A$
has operator norm at most $L_A$ by its squared matrix-entry sum.
Its constrained image uses $2d$ continuous coordinates.

For $R\ge1$, $0<\epsilon\le1$, quantize $|X^J|\le R$,
$|U^0|\le V_c$ at Euclidean error at most $\epsilon$, retaining
$I$ exactly. Send the outside event to $(0,0,I)$.
Its average squared cost is at most
$\epsilon^2+T_PR^{-(P-2)}$.
There are at most $J_PR^d\epsilon^{-2d}$ representatives including
the two tail representatives, of squared diameter at most $D_P^2R^2$.

For independent, possibly nonidentically distributed rows, the
sum of all empirical cell-mass variances is at most $1/N$:
each row's one-hot vector has summed variance at most one.
Cauchy--Schwarz gives expected TV from the mean quantized law
at most half the square root of cell count over $N$.
The squared three-term transport triangle consequently gives

$$
E W_2^2\le6\epsilon^2+6T_PR^{-(P-2)}
 +\frac32D_P^2\sqrt{J_P}R^{2+d/2}\epsilon^{-d}N^{-1/2}.
$$

With $\epsilon=N^{-b_P/2}$ and $R=N^{b_P/(P-2)}$, all three
exponents equal $-b_P$. The linear map contributes $L_A^2$.
:::

:::{prf:theorem} Actual stationary post-jitter and B1 quadratic transport rate
:label: thm-nqg-preparation-rate

Let $E_1=2t\nu V_c$ be the original coefficient of (NQC.7), and put

$$
\mathcal R_{A,N}=3[(t^2+1+b^2)E_1^2/N
 +L_A^2C_{{\rm sam},P}N^{-b_P}
 +C_\Gamma^2B_{{\rm prep},N}].
\tag{NQG.9}
$$

Then

$$
E_{\nu_N}W_{2,A}(\eta_N,\eta_*)^2\le\mathcal R_{A,N},
\qquad E_{\nu_N}W_{2,A}(\eta_N,\eta_*)\le\sqrt{\mathcal R_{A,N}}.
\tag{NQG.10}
$$

Its expected quadratic rate is $O(N^{-a_p/2})$ with all original
low-alive and survival terms retained. At $d=3,p=4$ this is
$O(N^{-2/35})$. Global normalization, shared component Haar
variables and the actual first-kick correlations are included.
:::

:::{prf:proof}
Freeze the compact record and compare the actual first count kick
with the reference of (NQG.8), using the same own jitter.
The already proved first-kick comparison (NQC.10) gives
$N^{-1}\sum_iE|U_i-U_i^0|^2\le E_1^2/N$.
Its own jitter is retained in the conditional sampling variance.
Its reference self term is zero since the own $w_i$ is fixed.
No independence of the actual $U_i$ is needed.
The paired $X_i^J,I_i$ agree, so their squared $A$ discrepancy is
$(t^2+1+b^2)|U_i-U_i^0|^2$.
Use that labelled coupling, then (NQG.8), then (NQG.7) with
$\Theta'=\Theta_*$. The squared three-term triangle and (NQG.4)
give (NQG.9)--(NQG.10); Cauchy--Schwarz gives the expected rate.
The reference laws are proof couplings and do not modify the gas.
:::

(sec-nqg-dense)=
## 4. The same preparation density and complete B2 force

:::{prf:definition} Common-stage fields and primitive smoothing constants
:label: def-nqg-dense-fields

For a physical preparation projection $\eta$, use the original
Gaussian density $\varphi_\tau$ and put

$$
\rho_\eta(x)=\int\varphi_\tau(x-m)\,\eta(dA),\qquad
\Lambda_2(\eta)=\operatorname{Law}(m+tqZ,cv_1+qZ).
\tag{NQG.11}
$$

The same original $Z$ occurs in both stage coordinates.
For a multiindex $\beta$, set
$L_{\tau,\beta}=\sup_x|\partial^\beta\varphi_\tau(x)|$ and
$L_{\tau,\beta+1}=\sup_x|\nabla\partial^\beta\varphi_\tau(x)|$.
For example $L_{\tau,0}=(2\pi\tau^2)^{-d/2}$ and
$L_{\tau,1}=L_{\tau,0}e^{-1/2}/\tau$.
Higher constants are finite Hermite-polynomial/Gaussian suprema.
They are primitive polynomial maximizations, rather than unknown
stationary regularity constants.
The native count fields use their complete common-stage bulk:

$$
\alpha_\eta(X)=\int K_\rho(X-X')\,d\Lambda_2(\eta),\quad
\beta_\eta(X)=\int K_\rho(X-X')z'\,d\Lambda_2(\eta),\quad
F_\eta(X,z)=\nu[\beta_\eta(X)-\alpha_\eta(X)z].
\tag{NQG.12}
$$

Retain $Z_2=cV_c+ct\lambda(R_D+\sigma_J\sqrt d)+q\sqrt d$.
:::

:::{prf:theorem} Quantitative stationary density and common-stage force
:label: thm-nqg-dense-fields

For every $\beta$ and each compact force-input velocity radius $R_z$,

$$
E\|\partial^\beta\rho_{\eta_N}-\partial^\beta\rho_{\eta_*}\|_\infty
\le L_{\tau,\beta+1}\sqrt{\mathcal R_{A,N}},
$$
$$
E W_2(\Lambda_2(\eta_N),\Lambda_2(\eta_*))^2
\le\mathcal R_{A,N},
$$
$$
E\sup_{X,\ |z|\le R_z}|F_{\eta_N}(X,z)-F_{\eta_*}(X,z)|
\le\nu[1+\ell_\rho(Z_2+R_z)]\sqrt{\mathcal R_{A,N}}.
\tag{NQG.13}
$$

These are explicit $N^{-a_p/2}$ rates for dense fields attached
to the actual stationary preparation $\eta_N$.
:::

:::{prf:proof}
Couple $\eta,\eta_*$ in $d_A$ at quadratic cost $\epsilon^2$.
The mean value theorem gives the density bound, because $m$ is
retained in that metric. Using the same original OU row gives
squared stage discrepancy
$|m-m'|^2+c^2|v_1-v_1'|^2\le d_A^2$.
It does not factorize $X$ and $z$.

In this stage coupling,
$|\alpha_\eta-\alpha_{\eta_*}|\le\ell_\rho\epsilon$.
Split $\beta$ into a velocity discrepancy and a kernel discrepancy
multiplied by the reference source velocity.
Cauchy--Schwarz gives
$|\beta_\eta-\beta_{\eta_*}|\le(1+\ell_\rho Z_2)\epsilon$.
Equation (NQG.12) proves the stated count factor, uniformly in $X$.
Use (NQG.10) and Cauchy--Schwarz over the stationary record.
Gaussian density derivatives are bounded globally.
:::

(sec-nqg-posterior)=
## 5. Quantitative posterior comparison on the existing reset phase

:::{prf:definition} Derived reset localization constants
:label: def-nqg-reset-localization

For the following uniform marked-window estimates use the existing
reset parameter relation

$$
a_x=0,\qquad \lambda=[t^2(1+c)]^{-1}.
\tag{NQG.14}
$$

It includes the positive primitive contraction witness.
Its original first kick has $m=bU$ and $|m|\le M_m=bV_c$
for every prepared row. For a fixed terminal query radius $R_x$,
define

$$
h_\tau=(2\pi\tau^2)^{-d/2},\quad
d_x=h_\tau e^{-(R_x+M_m)^2/(2\tau^2)}>0,\quad
L_\psi=L_{\tau,1}[d_x^{-1}+h_\tau d_x^{-2}].
\tag{NQG.15}
$$

Both $\rho_{\eta_N}(x),\rho_{\eta_*}(x)\ge d_x$ on
$|x|\le R_x$, deterministically over the actual preparation.
This follows from the bounded reset center, rather than an
assumption on the stationary density.
The actual posterior marks use the computed original residual $E$:

$$
\pi_{\eta,x}(dA,dE)
=\frac{\varphi_\tau(x-m)}{\rho_\eta(x)}\eta(dA)\gamma_d(dE),
$$
$$
z=cv_1+a_Y(x-m)+q\sqrt\chi E,\quad X=p_1+tz,\quad
a_Y=tq^2/\tau^2,\quad\chi=s^2/\tau^2.
\tag{NQG.16}
$$
:::

:::{prf:lemma} Actual posterior marked coupling
:label: lem-nqg-posterior-coupling

For admitted reset preparations $\eta,\eta'$ and $|x|\le R_x$,
put $\epsilon=W_{2,A}(\eta,\eta')$.
Their posterior coupling has unmatched probability at most
$\min\{1,L_\psi\epsilon\}$.
On its matched submeasure use the same original residual $E$;
its expected $d_A$ cost is at most $(h_\tau/d_x)\epsilon$.
The corresponding stage discrepancy obeys

$$
E_{\rm matched}(|X-X'|+|z-z'|)
\le C_{\rm post}\epsilon,\qquad
C_{\rm post}=(h_\tau/d_x)[1+(1+t)(c+a_Y)].
\tag{NQG.17}
$$
:::

:::{prf:proof}
In a $d_A$-optimal coupling $\Gamma$, let
$w=\varphi_\tau(x-m)/\rho_\eta(x)$ and
$w'=\varphi_\tau(x-m')/\rho_{\eta'}(x)$.
Their integrated absolute difference is at most $L_\psi\epsilon$:
the numerator changes by $L_{\tau,1}|m-m'|$ and the denominator
by at most $L_{\tau,1}\epsilon$, with both denominators at least
$d_x$ and both numerators at most $h_\tau$.
Use $\min(w,w')\Gamma$ as the matched subcoupling and complete
its remaining marginals arbitrarily. Its $d_A$ cost is bounded
by $(h_\tau/d_x)E_\Gamma d_A$.
Attach a common independent residual $E$ on this part.
Equation (NQG.16) gives
$|\Delta z|\le(c+a_Y)d_A$ and
$|\Delta X|\le[1+t(c+a_Y)]d_A$, proving (NQG.17).
:::

:::{prf:theorem} Quantitative same-record bounded marked-window comparison
:label: thm-nqg-marked-window

Fix a bounded spatial window $W$ in rescaled coordinates and
$|x|\le R_x$. Use the complete comparison Poisson process of
{prf:ref}`thm-nmg-marked-poisson`, with intensity
$du\,\varphi_\tau(x-m)\eta(dA)\gamma_d(dE)$, and retain its
root posterior $\pi_{\eta,x}$.
At a neighbor point $u$ keep its actual physical query
$y=x+N^{-1/d}u$ in (NQG.16) and in its force/readout.
The frozen intensity uses $x$ as in (NMG.4); this comparison
does not freeze the neighbor's native stage coordinate at $x$.
Let $H$ be bounded and, at fixed unmarked configuration, Lipschitz
with coefficient $L_H$ in the sum of $d_A$ discrepancies of its
root and retained marks. It may be any measurable function of the
same unmarked geometric configuration, including adjacent stars.
Its conditional comparison expectation $\Phi_{H,W}(\eta,x)$ obeys
the following bound with the observation function $H$ held fixed.
Any separate dense-force dependence of that function on its varying
environment is paid explicitly in (NQG.28), rather than absorbed
into this fixed-test statement:

$$
|\Phi_{H,W}(\eta,x)-\Phi_{H,W}(\eta',x)|
\le C_{H,W} W_{2,A}(\eta,\eta'),
$$
$$
C_{H,W}=2\|H\|_\infty[L_\psi+|W|L_{\tau,1}]
                   +L_H[h_\tau/d_x+|W|h_\tau].
\tag{NQG.18}
$$

Thus its expected stationary environment error is at most
$C_{H,W}\sqrt{\mathcal R_{A,N}}$, uniformly on the query ball.
The conditional finite native marked process adds

$$
2\|H\|_\infty[h_\tau^2|W|^2/N+
 N^{-1/d}L_{\tau,1}\int_W|u|\,du+h_\tau|W|/N].
\tag{NQG.19}
$$

The last term restores its one excluded root in the intensity.
At a fixed addressed root the actual mark remains $A_i$.
The posterior-root statement applies to the root averaged with
its original terminal-density weights in an assembled observation.
:::

:::{prf:proof}
Lift an optimal $A$ coupling to paired intensity measures and
couple points through their common sub-intensity
$\min\{\varphi_\tau(x-m),\varphi_\tau(x-m')\}
\Gamma(dA,dA')\,du$.
Matched spatial points agree exactly; their residual marks use
the same $E$. The expected unmatched count is at most
$|W|L_{\tau,1}\epsilon$, and matched summed $d_A$ cost at most
$|W|h_\tau\epsilon$.
Couple the root as in the preceding lemma. On any mismatch
pay $2\|H\|_\infty$, otherwise its stipulated mark continuity.
This gives (NQG.18). All geometric functions use exactly the
same unmarked configuration on the successful coupling; no
continuity of retessellation has been assumed.

For the actual conditional terminal rows apply (NMG.5).
Restoring the excluded root has intensity mass at most
$h_\tau|W|/N$. The posterior-root mixture is (NQG.16) precisely
when the root is mixed with its actual terminal-density weights.
The fixed addressed root is retained separately as stipulated.
:::

(sec-nqg-band)=
## 6. A primitive band bound for the actual hard color threshold

:::{prf:definition} Reset count threshold-band coefficients
:label: def-nqg-band-budget

Retain the existing reset count phase, its finite threshold
$\delta_c\ge0$, and the original $\nu,t,q,\rho>0$.
Write $B=\rho^2+t^2q^2$, $C_B=(\rho^2/B)^{d/2}$,
$k=\rho^2/B$, $M_m=bV_c$, and

$$
A_v=V_c+t\lambda R_D,\quad B_v=t\lambda\sigma_J,\quad
a_v=[8(1+B_v^2)]^{-1},\quad
M_v=e^{2a_vA_v^2}(1-4a_vB_v^2)^{-d/2},\quad
\overline v=V_c+t\lambda(R_D+\sigma_Jg_1).
\tag{NQG.20}
$$

For finite proof radii $R_p,R_z\ge1$, define a real witness radius
$r_0$ and positive witness value $g_0$ as follows.
At $\delta_c=0$, put

$$
D_0=c\sqrt{\frac{2M_mR_p/B+M_m^2/(2B)+\log M_v}{a_v}}
                    +\frac{tq^2}{B}(R_p+M_m),\quad
D_1=c\sqrt{\frac{2M_mt}{Ba_v}},
$$
$$
r_0=\max\{1,4D_0/k,(4D_1/k)^2,4/(\nu k)\},\qquad
g_0=4C_B^2 e^{-(R_p+tr_0+M_m)^2/B}.
\tag{NQG.21}
$$

At $\delta_c>0$, take $r_0$ to be the first integer
$r\ge1+(R_p+M_m)/t$ for which

$$
\nu C_B e^{-(tr-R_p-M_m)^2/(2B)}
\left[c\overline v+\frac{tq^2}{B}(R_p+tr+M_m)+r\right]
\le\delta_c/2,\qquad g_0=3\delta_c^2/4 .
\tag{NQG.22}
$$

The integer search is finite by Gaussian decay.
Set $R_{\rm dom}=\max\{R_z,r_0\}+2$ and

$$
\begin{gathered}
M_F=\nu C_B e^{t^2/(2B)}
\left[c\overline v+\frac{tq^2}{B}
 (R_p+tR_{\rm dom}+M_m+t)+R_{\rm dom}+1\right],\\
M=\max\{1,M_F^2+\delta_c^2,g_0\},\qquad
J=\lceil4(R_z+r_0)\rceil+1,\qquad
\gamma=M(g_0/M)^{2^J},\\
n=\max\{1,\lceil\log_4(4M/(3\gamma))\rceil\},\qquad
\gamma_1=\gamma/[4(n+1)],\\
M_{\rm der}=\sqrt d\,d^{n/2}(n+1)!(2\sqrt d)^{n+1}M,\qquad
h_1=\min\{1/4,\gamma_1/(4M_{\rm der})\},\\
N_{\rm cov}=(1+4R_z/h_1)^d,\qquad
C_n=\max_{1\le j\le n}2(j+1)[2j!(j+1)]^{1/j}.
\end{gathered}
\tag{NQG.23}
$$

All these are strictly positive finite primitive quantities.
Their logarithmic definitions avoid floating-point underflow:
$\log\gamma=\log M+2^J(\log g_0-\log M)$.
Let $v_{d-1}$ be the unit $(d-1)$-ball volume, with $v_0=1$.
The original residual posterior density upper bound is
$H_z=(2\pi q^2\chi)^{-d/2}$.
:::

:::{prf:lemma} Derived compact threshold band for the native count force
:label: lem-nqg-compact-threshold-band

For every own $|p_1|\le R_p$ and every original residual posterior
with that own preparation, the population field of $\eta_*$ satisfies

$$
\Pr\bigl(\big||F_{\eta_*}(p_1+tz,z)|-\delta_c\big|\le\epsilon,
                      \ |z|\le R_z\mid A,x\bigr)
\le H_zN_{\rm cov}v_{d-1}h_1^{d-1}C_n
       \left[\frac{(2\delta_c+1)\epsilon}{\gamma_1}\right]^{1/n}
\tag{NQG.24}
$$

for $0<\epsilon\le\min\{1,\gamma_1/[2(2\delta_c+1)]\}$.
No nondegeneracy hypothesis on the unknown stationary color law
is consumed.
:::

:::{prf:proof}
The actual population preparation has
$|v_1|\le A_v+B_v|Z^J|$ and its retained jitter marginal is
standard Gaussian, even though $U$ depends on that jitter.
Thus $\eta_*e^{a_v|v_1|^2}\le M_v$ directly from (NQG.20).
The exact Gaussian square gives, for $X=p_1+tz$,

$$
F_{\eta_*}(X,z)
=\nu C_B\int e^{-|X-m|^2/(2B)}
\left[cv_1+\frac{tq^2}{B}(X-m)-z\right]\,d\eta_* .
\tag{NQG.25}
$$

Since $|m|\le M_m$, its exponential tilt has entropy at most
$2M_m|X|/B+M_m^2/(2B)$ relative to $\eta_*$.
Indeed factor out $e^{-|X|^2/(2B)}$; the logarithm of the
remaining weight has oscillation at most that amount.
The elementary entropy/Jensen inequality consequently gives

$$
E_X|v_1|\le
\sqrt{[2M_m|X|/B+M_m^2/(2B)+\log M_v]/a_v}.
$$

At $z=r_0u$ for a unit $u$ and $|p_1|\le R_p$, its row vector's
$u$ projection is at most $\nu(D_0+D_1\sqrt{r_0}-kr_0)$.
The choice (NQG.21) makes this at most $-\nu kr_0/2\le-2$.
The count degree is at least
$C_Be^{-(R_p+tr_0+M_m)^2/(2B)}$, hence the squared count force
at this real witness is at least $g_0$ when $\delta_c=0$.
For $\delta_c>0$, (NQG.25), $|m|\le M_m$ and
$\eta_*|v_1|\le\overline v$ give exactly (NQG.22)'s upper
bound. Its witness squared force differs from $\delta_c^2$
in absolute value by at least $g_0$.

For real $p_1$, the scalar
$g(z)=\sum_aF_{\eta_*,a}(p_1+tz,z)^2-\delta_c^2$
extends to an entire function of the complex $z$ coordinates.
Dominated differentiation follows from $|m|\le M_m$ and the
integrable $|v_1|$. In the complex Euclidean tube
$|\operatorname{Re}z|\le R_{\rm dom}$,
$|\operatorname{Im}z|\le1$, (NQG.25) bounds $|F|\le M_F$,
so $|g|\le M$. At the real witness $r_0e_1$ its modulus is
at least $g_0$, uniformly over the own $p_1$.

Fix any real $z$ in the $R_z$ ball. Along the unit direction
from $z$ to that witness, use discs of radii $1/4,1/2,1$
and a chain of at most $J$ steps of length at most $1/4$.
The maximum modulus on the middle disc is at most the square
root of the product of the inner and outer maxima.
For completeness this three-circle bound follows by applying
the maximum principle to
$\log|g|$ minus the affine function of $\log|w|$ agreeing
with the two boundary log maxima on their annulus; zeros are
handled by the usual limiting value $-\infty$.
A next inner disc is contained in the preceding middle disc.
Iteration therefore implies that the first inner maximum is
at least $\gamma=M(g_0/M)^{2^J}$.

The unit-disc Cauchy bound gives a Taylor tail on radius $1/4$
after order $n$ at most $M/(3\,4^n)\le\gamma/4$.
If every weighted derivative through that order were at most
$\gamma/[4(n+1)]$, the full inner maximum would be at most
$\gamma/2$, a contradiction. Some directional derivative of
order $j\le n$ therefore has modulus at least $\gamma_1$
at $z$, because $j!(1/4)^{-j}\ge1$.

Multivariable Cauchy on polydiscs of radius $1/(2\sqrt d)$
bounds the gradient of every such directional derivative by
$M_{\rm der}$. Its order expansion has coefficient sum at most
$d^{j/2}$; each additional coordinate derivative costs at most
$(j+1)!(2\sqrt d)^{j+1}$. The tube above contains all these
discs. Cover the real $R_z$ ball by at most $N_{\rm cov}$
balls of radius $h_1$, centered in that ball. At each center
choose its established direction and order. On that whole
ball the same derivative has modulus at least $\gamma_1/2$.
For order zero, no $|g|\le\gamma_1/2$ event occurs.

For order $j\ge1$, slice that ball into intervals in its
chosen direction. If a real sublevel set $|g|\le u$ on an
interval has length $\ell$, choose $j+1$ of its points
separated by at least $\ell/[2(j+1)]$; a greedy selection
works since $j$ deleted intervals have smaller total length.
Their divided difference is at most
$(j+1)u[2(j+1)/\ell]^j$ in modulus.
Repeated Rolle's theorem identifies it with
$g^{(j)}(\xi)/j!$, whose modulus is at least
$\gamma_1/(2j!)$. Hence
$\ell\le C_n(u/\gamma_1)^{1/n}$ for $u/\gamma_1\le1$.
Fubini over the cross-section and the finite cover give
Lebesgue measure at most
$N_{\rm cov}v_{d-1}h_1^{d-1}C_n(u/\gamma_1)^{1/n}$.
Finally the force-norm band implies
$|g|\le(2\delta_c+1)\epsilon$ for $\epsilon\le1$.
Multiply that volume by the actual Gaussian posterior density
bound $H_z$ to obtain (NQG.24).

:::{prf:definition} Smooth bounded local observation budget

The actual projector of $c_a=F_ae^{i\kappa_cz_a}/|F|$ has
Frobenius derivative at most $4/|F|$ in $F$ and $2|\kappa_c|$

:::{prf:theorem} Quantitative stationary smooth marked-window observation
:label: thm-nqg-smooth-observation

Use the reset phase and query ball above.
Let $\Phi_{H_N,W}(\eta_*,x)$ keep every exact geometric scale at
that same $N$, on its common marked Poisson configuration and
complete population force, evaluating a neighbor stage at its
actual $y=x+N^{-1/d}u$. Let $\mathcal F$ be the original complete
prepared array and $I_{\rm root}$ an independent uniform root
index. Define the exact conditional expectation

$$
\mathcal A_N(\mathcal F,x)
=E[H_N(\hbox{actual root and marked window})
                         \mid\mathcal F,Y_{I_{\rm root}}=x].
$$

Within this fixed preparation the root index is tilted by its
own original terminal density; the preparation itself is held
fixed. Expectation over the original unconditioned preparation law
then satisfies

$$
E_{\mathcal F}
|\mathcal A_N(\mathcal F,x)-\Phi_{H_N,W}(\eta_*,x)|
\le
C_{H,W}^{\rm env}\sqrt{\mathcal R_{A,N}}
 +L_F(1+h_\tau|W|)\sqrt{C_F/N}
 +2B_H[h_\tau^2|W|^2/N+
 N^{-1/d}L_{\tau,1}\int_W|u|\,du+h_\tau|W|/N],
\tag{NQG.29}
$$

The right side of (NQG.29) thus bounds this displayed absolute
conditional-expectation discrepancy. For conditioning the entire
stationary raw law on its uniform terminal root $Y_{I_{\rm root}}=x$,
multiply that bound by $h_\tau/d_x$. Indeed its preparation law
is tilted by $\rho_{\eta_N}(x)/E\rho_{\eta_N}(x)$, which is at most
$h_\tau/d_x$. Both interpretations retain their exact root law.

A larger primitive coefficient is

$$
V_1=V_c+t\lambda(R_D+\sigma_J\sqrt d),\qquad
C_F=128(1+d)\nu^2[dq^2+(1+h_\tau/d_x)c^2V_1^2
 +a_Y^2(R_x+1+M_m)^2+q^2\chi d].
\tag{NQG.30}
$$

Require $N^{-1/d}\sup_{u\in W}|u|\le1$.
For an unconditioned bounded root/window observation the actual
surviving output adds at most $2B_H(1-a_0)^N$.
For the whole stationary law conditioned at the fixed root $x$,
the corresponding selection term is instead

$$

[\Phi((L_D-M_m)/\tau)-\Phi((-L_D-M_m)/\tau)]^d>0 .
$$

Thus its point-conditioned bound is $h_\tau/d_x$ times

A fixed-window smooth observation thus has error

:::{prf:proof}

$1+h_\tau|W|$. Cauchy--Schwarz bounds summed force errors by

array, not independently sampled complete output rows.

For the common-field comparison, first change its environment
with marks held fixed. Equation (NQG.13) gives

The proof has bounded the absolute conditional discrepancy before

For global terminal-root conditioning, Bayes' formula gives the

:::{prf:corollary} Quantitative transfer of the literal hard color masks
:label: cor-nqg-hard-observation

\tag{NQG.32}

:::{prf:proof}
