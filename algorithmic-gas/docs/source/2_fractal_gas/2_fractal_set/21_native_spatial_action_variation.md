(sec-nsav-complete-register)=
## 1. The actual ray-action instrument

:::{prf:definition} Complete native ray-action data
:label: def-nsav-complete-register

Retain every execution and consumed observation field of
{prf:ref}`def-nspc-complete-register` and
{prf:ref}`def-npg-complete-record`: algorithm/variant, landscape and
provider, all fitness and normalization parameters, donor pools and
their simultaneous assignment law, clone gates, revival, jitter,
component collision marks, both kicks, independent real Gaussian
innovations, domain and boundary schedule, actual cap, history
coverage, event identities, finite-value/eligible masks, precision,
budgets and error policies. The positive results below specify included
parameter regimes of this complete record. They do not replace the
unchanged positive-final-noise reference by those regimes.

The algorithm tag for every positive dense count/row theorem is
the EXISTING canonical real-coordinate dense viscous gas. Its
force is the one in Rust `QftExecutionConfig.viscosity`, evaluated
by `src/kinetic.rs`'s `recorded_force`: every eligible nonself
Gaussian pair is retained; count normalization uses eligible
count, and `row_normalized=true` uses its actual positive row
mass, with no $10^{-12}$ denominator floor. Its only zero-mass
branch is the executed zero-force branch. Graph-viscosity
feedback is not configured in this tag. The original archive
ray instrument below applies to this algorithm's recorded states.
The Python CSR/Delaunay force variant has its different graph
and mass-floor parameters. In particular its $N=2,d=3$ empty
graph fallback is not assigned this dense two-row force or swap.
Floating Gaussian underflow, finite streams and numerical
error branches keep their actual zero-mass/rounded outcomes;
the global exact row cancellations below concern the specified
real-coordinate execution tag, where $K_\rho>0$.

The literal action is the archive instrument in
`algorithmic-gas/crates/algorithmic-gas/src/physics/qft/run_observables.rs`,
experiments $1,11,15,23,24,29$. It constructs, from the actual
`before` and `final_population` arrays,

$$
r_e=\frac{x_e+iv_e}{\sqrt{|x_e|^2+|v_e|^2}},\qquad
\ell_{ab}=\frac{r_a^\dagger r_b}{|r_a^\dagger r_b|},\qquad
L_T=\ell_{ab}\ell_{bc}\ell_{ca},\qquad W_T=1-\Re L_T.
\tag{NSAV.1}
$$

The original eligible test uses `include_truncated`; the ray norm
must strictly exceed $\delta_r=10^{-12}$ and be finite, and each
normalized overlap must strictly exceed $\delta_\ell=10^{-10}$.
Unavailable triangles are counted and omitted from the loop/action
arrays. An unavailable phase is not assigned a value by this instrument.
The graph is the actual `FractalSet::from_archive` graph: a current
companion produces the ordered triangle
$(\mathrm{pre}\ i,\mathrm{final}\ i,\mathrm{pre}\ j)$,
with CST, IA and reversed IG boundary. IA and this triangle exist
also when the recorded clone was not accepted. Identical distance and
cloning triangles are deduplicated, with both channels retained.
Historical/unresolved/self sources retain the literal graph rules;
they are not replaced by current numerical slots. There is no CSR
three-final-vertex triangle in this archive action.

Each returned $W_T$ has its literal unit coefficient. The output
contains per-face values rather than a newly weighted probability
density. A sum of these actual values below is explicitly such an
observable. The `angle` control only chooses the local rephasing
comparison, and the numerical phase-variation comparison uses the
fixed step $10^{-5}$. Neither changes the original $L_T$ or supplies
an algorithmic source. The matched B2 color coefficient
$\kappa=m\ell_0/\hbar_{\mathrm{eff}}$, physical coordinate units,
and a covariance-metric ridge do not enter (NSAV.1). Their own
instruments remain in the complete record; they are not inserted
into this raw-component ray formula.

The original cap is
$C_V(w)=Vw/(V+|w|)$ for its configured finite $V>0$, and
$C_\infty(w)=w$ when `velocity_cap=None`.
The results using real densities and conditional Gaussian fibers
refer to the real independent-Gaussian execution convention.
Fixed-stream and rounded executions retain their actual pushforward
laws and are not assigned these continuous densities.

(sec-nsav-original-first-variation)=
## 3. The original interaction-face first variation

:::{prf:theorem} Exact ray-face derivative and original Gaussian weak score
:label: thm-nsav-native-source-variation

On any originally available face and smooth source branch,
let $Q_{ab}=r_a^\dagger r_b$. For its actual simultaneous
source tangent $\delta$,

$$
\delta\Theta_T=
 \Im\sum_{(a,b)\in\partial T}
 \frac{(\delta r_a)^\dagger r_b+r_a^\dagger\delta r_b}{Q_{ab}},
\qquad
\delta W_T=\sin\Theta_T\,\delta\Theta_T.
\tag{NSAV.6}
$$

Here $\Theta_T=\arg L_T$ may be locally lifted; its
formula is independent of that lift. At a persisted slot
with fixed original real pre rays $a,b$, it is equivalently

$$
\delta\Theta_T=
\Im\left[
 \frac{a\cdot(\delta Y+i\delta u)}{a\cdot(Y+iu)}+
 \frac{b\cdot(\delta Y-i\delta u)}{b\cdot(Y-iu)}
\right].
\tag{NSAV.7}
$$

For a fixed complete pre-O preparation on a full-eligible
smooth quadratic $\sigma_x=0$ B2 branch, write $z_i=cv_{1i}+qG_i$,
$Y_i=p_i+tz_i$, and
$w_i=z_i-t\lambda Y_i+tF_i(Y,z)$. The ORIGINAL source
$G_i\mapsto G_i+\theta f_i$ has

$$
\delta z_i=qf_i,\quad \delta Y_i=tqf_i,\quad
\delta w_i=(1-t^2\lambda)qf_i+t\delta F_i,\quad
\delta u_i=DC_V(w_i)\delta w_i.
\tag{NSAV.8}
$$

Put $\Delta z_{ij}=z_j-z_i$, $\Delta Y_{ij}=Y_j-Y_i$
and $K_{ij}=e^{-|\Delta Y_{ij}|^2/(2\rho^2)}$.
The fully coupled count derivative is
$\delta F_i=(\nu/N)\sum_{j\ne i}K_{ij}
[\delta\Delta z_{ij}-\rho^{-2}\Delta z_{ij}
(\Delta Y_{ij}\cdot\delta\Delta Y_{ij})]$.
For row normalization replace $N$ by
$D_i=\sum_{j\ne i}K_{ij}$ and add
$-F_i\delta D_i/D_i$, with
$\delta D_i=-\rho^{-2}\sum_{j\ne i}K_{ij}
\Delta Y_{ij}\cdot\delta\Delta Y_{ij}$.
Thus all simultaneous $\delta z_j$ and actual prepared
position differences are kept. Other fixed eligibility
strata retain their literal masked row sums. For $w\ne0$,

$$
DC_V(w)=\frac{V}{V+|w|}I
 -\frac{Vww^T}{|w|(V+|w|)^2},\qquad DC_V(0)=I.
\tag{NSAV.9}
$$

For every bounded measurable observable $O$ of the COMPLETE
executed output, including all retained mask/retessellation
branches, its original weak source identity is

$$
\left.\partial_\theta E O(D(G+\theta f))\right|_0
 =E\left[O(D(G))\sum_i f_i\cdot G_i\right].
\tag{NSAV.10}
$$

The exact coarse likelihood score is
$E[\sum_i f_i\cdot G_i\mid D]$. Formula (NSAV.6)
is the actual smooth-stratum action-observable variation;
it is not a replacement for this coarse score or for
its threshold boundary contributions. The original
experiment-24 comparison perturbs one supplied edge
phase, for which its derivative is $\Im L_T$; that
comparison is distinct from the coupled source (NSAV.8).
:::

:::{prf:proof}
The differential of the argument of any nonzero complex
$Q$ is $\Im(dQ/Q)$. Its positive real modulus
normalization contributes no argument, so the loop
product rule gives (NSAV.6). Positive normalization of
the final ray also contributes no argument. The two
variable overlaps with real fixed pre rays are
$a\cdot(Y+iu)$ and $b\cdot(Y-iu)$, proving (NSAV.7).
The unchanged BAOAB update and original radial cap
give (NSAV.8)--(NSAV.9) by differentiation. In particular
the capped derivative includes its radial term; the
viscous derivative includes every other source row and
the row denominator derivative.

Freeze the complete preparation, including its actual
cloning, jitter and shared collision marks. Change variables
in the independent standard Gaussian integral for
$G+\theta f$. The shifted density is
$\varphi(G-\theta f)$, whose derivative at zero is
$\varphi(G)\sum_i f_i\cdot G_i$. Its local derivatives
are bounded by an integrable Gaussian times a polynomial,
so bounded $O$ gives differentiation under the integral.
The observation executes the same complete native map
under the shifted source, including all hard branches.
Average over the unchanged preparation law. Disintegration
against $D$ gives its coarse score. For an independent
edge-phase perturbation $L_T\mapsto e^{i\theta}L_T$,
direct differentiation gives $\partial_\theta W_T=\Im L_T$;
it does not differentiate the force, positions or cap.
:::

\tag{NSAV.14}

\begin{pmatrix}

\tag{NSAV.15}
