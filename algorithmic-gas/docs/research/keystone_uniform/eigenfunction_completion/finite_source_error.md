# Finite-population second-generation native source producer

This account uses the native record and full joint laws in
establishment.md, (KULN.11)--(KULN.15). It quantifies the actual
second reward/diversity registers, first B2 fields and source degree
by conditioning each tagged row's own original Gaussian variables.
The signed incoming-minus-outgoing identity is evaluated on the
actual measured array. The finite threshold below favors a
transparent proof over a useful numerical population size.

The result is a two-update producer. It does not iterate its
output class or establish the global right-eigenfunction ratio.

## 1. Conditional empirical means and unrestricted tails

For independent vector variables \(H_1,\ldots,H_n\) with the same
law and finite second moment,
\[
 \mathbb E\left|\frac1n\sum_jH_j-\mathbb EH\right|
 \le\sqrt{\frac{\mathbb E|H|^2}{n}}.
 \tag{KUFN.1}
\]
Expand the squared norm, cancel independent centered cross terms,
and apply Cauchy--Schwarz. This remains true conditional on an
independent tagged root: no tagged-root Gaussian is replaced by
an independent marginal.

The original Gaussian tails satisfy, by direct integration,
\[
 \Pr(|Z|>20)\le2\phi(20)/20,\qquad
 \mathbb E[|Z|{\bf1}_{|Z|>20}]=2\phi(20).
\]
For the three-dimensional original OU vector,
\[
 \Pr(\max_j|\xi_j|>20)\le6Q(20)<10^{-86}.
 \tag{KUFN.2}
\]
These tails remain part of the actual transition. Define
\[
 \rho_N=N^{-1/2}+10^{-80},\qquad N\ge4.
 \tag{KUFN.3}
\]
For \(N\ge10^{50}\), \(\rho_N<1.01\cdot10^{-25}\).

## 2. Actual first B2 fields and the joint source coupling

Start with the exact all-alive single-seed state: one position zero,
\(N-1\) positions \(e_1\), and every velocity zero. The actual sampled
fitness calculation (KULN.17) gives \(\mathbb E L_N<2.5\), where
\(L_N\) counts first accepted recipients. The exceptional set of
these recipients together with the seed has expected size below
\(3.5\).

Every original component-Haar collision and first viscous field is
identically zero. Couple an unchanged resident to its reference
using exactly its own original \(\xi_i,\zeta_i\). Its completed
position and alive/dead mark agree identically with
\(m_A+tq\xi_i+s\zeta_i\). Its velocity differs only through the
actual finite B2 field. Put
\[
 Y_i^A=m_A+tq\xi_i,\quad W_i^A=w_A+q\xi_i,\quad
 k_y(Y)=e^{-|y-Y|^2/2},
\]
\[
 A(y)=\mathbb E k_y(Y^A),\qquad
 B(y)=\mathbb E[k_y(Y^A)W^A].
 \tag{KUFN.4}
\]
These are the original pre-final-noise fields
(KULN.12)--(KULN.14). Since \(q<.2\), \(|w_A|<.04\),
\[
 \mathbb E|W^A|^2=|w_A|^2+3q^2<.122,\qquad
 \sqrt{\mathbb E|W^A|^2}<.35.
 \tag{KUFN.5}
\]

Condition on the tagged row's own \(\xi_i,\zeta_i\) and the entire
first frozen plan. The original Gaussian variables in every
other slot remain independent. For the ideal root-excluded
sample of \(N-1\) residents, (KUFN.1) bounds the conditional
kernel-denominator error by \(1/\sqrt{N-1}\), and the vector
numerator error by \(.35/\sqrt{N-1}\), uniformly in the
conditioned root \(y\).

An exceptional actual pre-B2 velocity is
\(W=ctF(X)+q\xi\), \(X\in\{0,e_1\}+.1I J\).
Its original \(J,\xi\) remain independent conditional on the first
plan, and
\[
 \mathbb E(|W|+|W^A|\mid\text{first plan})<4:
 \quad \mathbb E|X|\le1+.1\sqrt3,\quad
 |F(X)|\le2|X|+20\pi\sqrt3,\quad ct<.02.
 \tag{KUFN.6}
\]
Consequently replacing exceptional rows changes the expected
root-excluded kernel mean by at most \(3.5/(N-1)\) and the
numerator by at most \(14/(N-1)\). The tagged row itself is
excluded. Integrating its original noises and plan, let
\(A_N',B_N'\) denote actual root-excluded means, divided by
\(N-1\). Then
\[
 \mathbb E|A_N'-A|\le2N^{-1/2}+7/N,\qquad
 \mathbb E|B_N'-B|\le N^{-1/2}+28/N.
 \tag{KUFN.7}
\]
These bounds also hold multiplied by the event that the tagged
resident is unchanged, or the persistent seed event used below.
The first plan is independent of the later original Gaussian
arrays; its expected exceptional count, rather than a deterministic
cap on that count, is the quantity in (KUFN.7).

For original count normalization the B2 field is
\[
 \nu\frac{N-1}{N}(B_N'-w A_N').
 \tag{KUFN.8}
\]
Thus the divisor is the configured \(N\), and self interaction
vanishes identically. The factor \((N-1)/N\) adds at most \(11/N\)
to the unscaled comparison on roots \(|w|\le10\), because
\(|B-wA|\le\mathbb E|W^A|+|w|<11\).

For row normalization and \(|y|\le2\),
\[
 A(y)=(1+r^2)^{-3/2}e^{-|y-m_A|^2/[2(1+r^2)]}>.01.
 \tag{KUFN.9}
\]
On \(|A_N'-A|\le.005\), use the actual denominator lower bound
.005 and the conservative \(|B|<10\):
\[
 \left|\frac{B_N'}{A_N'}-\frac{B}{A}\right|
 \le200|B_N'-B|+200000|A_N'-A|.
 \tag{KUFN.10}
\]
On the complementary event the actual cap-feature discrepancy
is at most two, since \(|\phi_v|\le1\); Markov bounds its
probability by \(200\mathbb E|A_N'-A|\).
The B2-kick coefficient is \(t\nu=.006\), and the original
cap-feature composition \(w\mapsto w/(1+|w|)\) is one-Lipschitz.
For unchanged tagged residents with \(\max_j|\xi_{i,j}|\le20\),
\[
 |Y_i^A|<1.14<2,\qquad |W_i^A|<7<10.
\]
Equations (KUFN.7)--(KUFN.10) therefore bound their expected
velocity-feature discrepancy by
\[
 2\,\mathbb E|B_N'-B|+
 1600\,\mathbb E|A_N'-A|
 <10^5N^{-1/2}.
 \tag{KUFN.11}
\]
This deliberately enlarged coefficient covers both normalizations,
the count bias (KUFN.8), and \(N\ge4\). Outside that tagged OU
event use the bounded full feature discrepancy, at most five.
Exceptional rows also have feature discrepancy at most five.
It follows that
\[
 \mathbb E\frac1N\sum_i
 |\varphi(Z_i^{\rm actual})-\varphi(Z_i^A)|
 \le2\cdot10^5\rho_N.
 \tag{KUFN.12}
\]

The persistent seed never accepts. Its actual position agrees
identically with the reference \(X_B^+=tq\xi+s\zeta\). On
\[
 G_B=\{|X_B^+|\le r_*,\ |\xi|\le4.9\},\qquad
 r_*=\sqrt{.87/(1+20\pi^2)},
 \tag{KUFN.13}
\]
its pre-B2 root satisfies \(|y|<.02,\ |w|<1\).
The same conditional field estimate gives
\[
 \mathbb E[{\bf1}_{G_B}
 |\varphi(Z_B^{\rm actual})-\varphi(Z_B^0)|]
 \le10^5\rho_N.
 \tag{KUFN.14}
\]
Its own shared OU variable remains in the position, pre-B2 root,
velocity and source event throughout this calculation.

## 3. Actual live feature laws and changed reward registers

Let \(M\) be the actual first-output live count, and \(M^A\) the
reference resident live count. The marked discrepancies are
confined to exceptional rows. Since unchanged resident positions
and marks agree identically and \(\beta_A<10^{-520}\),
\[
 \mathbb E(1-M/N)\le\beta_A+3.5/N,\qquad
 \Pr(M<N/2)\le2\beta_A+7/N,\qquad
 \Pr(M^A<N/2)\le2\beta_A.
 \tag{KUFN.15}
\]
The full feature diameter is below five even on retained dead
positions. Matching the unchanged live pairs, using (KUFN.12),
and assigning unmatched exceptional live mass an arbitrary
feature of diameter five, gives
\[
 \mathbb E[{\bf1}_{M,M^A\ge N/2}
 W_1(\mu_N^a,\mu_N^{A,a})]<10^6\rho_N.
 \tag{KUFN.16}
\]
Here the comparison is between the actual and reference empirical
live feature measures. No empirical \(W_1\) estimate against a
continuous six-dimensional law is used. To check the constant,
renormalization of the matched pair mass costs at most four
times the unnormalized mean feature discrepancy, plus
\(20(3.5)/N\) for unmatched labels, which is covered by
\(10^6\rho_N\).

For rewards, \(-72\le R\le0\) on each actual live position.
Exceptional positions contribute at most \(72(3.5)/N\) to
the raw first moment and \(5184(3.5)/N\) to the second.
The iid reference raw first and second moment expected errors
are at most \(72/\sqrt N\) and \(5184/\sqrt N\). Divide by
\(M/N\ge1/2\), retain the live-count discrepancy, and use
\[
 |\sqrt{v+.01}-\sqrt{v'+.01}|\le5|v-v'|,
 \qquad |m^2-m'^2|\le144|m-m'|.
 \tag{KUFN.17}
\]
The expected total reward mean and regularized-standard-deviation
error on this event is below \(10^6\rho_N\). Its reference is
the changed actual live register (KULN.19), including the
retained correction (KULN.20)--(KULN.21).
Explicit collection of the raw moment, alive-count, division and
variance terms gives the stronger upper bound
\[
 \frac{1089648}{N}+\frac{155664}{\sqrt N}+311328\beta_A
 <10^6\rho_N,\qquad N\ge4.
\]

## 4. Measured diversity: conditional row means and shared registers

For alive features,
\[
 d(f,g)=\sqrt{|f-g|^2+10^{-6}}<5,\quad
 w(f,g)=e^{-|f-g|^2/8},
\]
\[
 |\Delta d|\le|\Delta f|+|\Delta g|,\quad
 |\Delta w|\le.31(|\Delta f|+|\Delta g|),\quad
 w\ge\kappa_a>.27.
 \tag{KUFN.18}
\]
For an alive feature law \(\mu\), define its exact with-self
weighted moment functional
\[
 F_j(\mu)=\int\mu(df)
 \frac{\int w(f,g)d(f,g)^j\mu(dg)}
      {\int w(f,g)\mu(dg)}.
 \tag{KUFN.19}
\]
Coupling both arguments and using denominators at least .27 gives
\[
 |F_1(\mu)-F_1(\nu)|\le66W_1(\mu,\nu),\qquad
 |F_2(\mu)-F_2(\nu)|\le348W_1(\mu,\nu).
 \tag{KUFN.20}
\]
For \(j=1\), the numerator Lipschitz constant is below
\(1+5(.31)<3\), and \(3/.27+5(.31)/.27^2<33\).
For \(j=2\), use \(10+25(.31)<18\), and
\(18/.27+25(.31)/.27^2<174\).
The two coupled arguments produce the displayed constants.
Removing an actual or reference diagonal changes either moment
by at most \(200/N\) on its live-count event: its removed
normalized mass in the with-self law is at most
\(1/(\kappa_a M)\le8/N\).

It remains to compare the reference empirical row law to the
actual marked reference integrals (KULN.22). Condition on one
reference alive root \(Z_i^A\). The other \(N-1\) full marked
reference slots remain iid. Its limiting alive-partner denominator
is at least \(\kappa_a(1-\beta_A)>.26\).
The conditional empirical errors in that denominator, and in
the \(j\)-moment numerator, are bounded by
\(1/\sqrt{N-1}\) and \(5^j/\sqrt{N-1}\), by (KUFN.1).
On denominator error at most .13 the sampled denominator is
at least .13 and its ratio error is at most
\[
 8|\Delta B_j|+8(5^j)|\Delta A|.
\]
On the complement the ratio discrepancy is at most \(5^j\),
and Markov's inequality contributes at most
\(8(5^j)\mathbb E|\Delta A|\).
Thus the conditional expected row-moment error is at most
\[
 24(5^j)/\sqrt{N-1}.
 \tag{KUFN.21}
\]
If the empirical alive-partner denominator is zero, use the
native singleton self-companion rule; its bounded value is
already covered by the bad-denominator event. Dead roots have
zero contribution and do not lose their marked coordinates.

Average these root errors and divide by \(M^A/N\ge1/2\).
For the limiting row function itself, its value is at most \(5^j\),
so the iid empirical root-average ratio has expected error at
most \(4(5^j)/\sqrt N\). Together these give expected first
and second diversity-moment errors at most
\[
 500/\sqrt N,\qquad 2500/\sqrt N
 \quad\text{on }M^A\ge N/2.
 \tag{KUFN.22}
\]
These estimates use expectation of the average row error;
they do not declare row errors independent.

Use (KUFN.16), (KUFN.20), both diagonal-removal accounts,
and (KUFN.22) to compare the actual conditional mean registers
with their full marked reference integrals. Finally, conditional
on the entire actual first output, the original second diversity
measurement marks are independent in \([0,5]\). The actually
sampled first and second moment registers differ in conditional
expected absolute value from their actual nonself conditional
means by at most \(8/\sqrt N\) and \(36/\sqrt N\).
The source's own mark is one of these original marks.

Use (KUFN.17), now with
\(|m^2-m'^2|\le10|m-m'|\) for diversity. Bad reference
live-count outcomes have probability at most \(2\beta_A\)
and bounded diversity-register cost below twenty.
The resulting complete account is
\[
 \mathbb E[{\bf1}_{M\ge N/2}
 (|\bar d_N-\bar d_\nu|+|s_{D,N}-s_{D,\nu}|)]
 <6\cdot10^9\rho_N.
 \tag{KUFN.23}
\]
For the coefficient, propagation of the mean and second-moment
errors through the regularized standard deviation gives
\((51\cdot66+5\cdot348)10^6=5.106\cdot10^9\).
The conditional row, sampled-mark and two diagonal-removal
terms contribute at most \(49788/\sqrt N\) after
\(N^{-1}\le(2\sqrt N)^{-1}\) for \(N\ge4\).
The bad-reference-live-count cost is at most \(40\beta_A\).
Their sum is below \(6\cdot10^9\rho_N\).
No expected diversity normalizer has been substituted for the
actually sampled shared normalizer.

## 5. Actual source degree and a single finite error account

For the persistent reference seed, condition on its original
\(\xi,\zeta\). The other \(N-1\) ideal residents are iid full
marked slots, so conditional empirical averaging bounds its
alive feature-weight degree error by \(25/\sqrt N\), including
normalization by the empirical alive count on \(M^A\ge N/2\).
Indeed the excluded-root alive denominator is at least \(1/4\)
for \(N\ge4\), yielding \(4|\Delta B|+4|\Delta A|\).
The bounded second moments give \(8/\sqrt{N-1}\); the coefficient twenty-five covers
root exclusion and \(N\ge4\).

Perturbing to the actual live empirical law uses (KUFN.16),
the source feature estimate (KUFN.14), and the .31-Lipschitz
Gaussian weight. The two root exclusions cost at most \(8/N\).
Let \(d_{N,B}\) and \(d_{\nu,B}\) denote actual and reference
nonself alive degrees. We obtain
\[
 \mathbb E[{\bf1}_{G_B,M\ge N/2}|d_{N,B}-d_{\nu,B}|]
 <4\cdot10^6\rho_N.
 \tag{KUFN.24}
\]
The source is alive on \(G_B\), since its actual position is
identical to the reference and \(r_*<2\).
The good-reference-count calculation uses \(M^A\ge N/2\).
On its complement, the difference of two degrees in \([0,1]\)
has expectation at most \(2\beta_A\), which is included above.
The complete source-degree account is bounded by
\[
 .31(10^5+10^6)\rho_N+25/\sqrt N+8/N+2\beta_A
 <4\cdot10^6\rho_N.
\]

Include \({\bf1}_{M<N/2}\), all four register errors on
\(M\ge N/2\), the source feature error on \(G_B\), and the degree
error on \(G_B,M\ge N/2\), in a nonnegative account \(E_N\).
Equations (KUFN.14)--(KUFN.24) imply
\[
 \mathbb E E_N<10^{12}\rho_N,\qquad
 \Pr(E_N>10^{-5})<1.01\cdot10^{-8}
 \quad(N\ge10^{50}).
 \tag{KUFN.25}
\]
The probability statement is Markov's inequality. Every changed
normalizer, both B2 normalizations, cap and alive gate has its
own preceding quantitative term.

## 6. Finite second completed basin descendants

The reference persistent-seed certificate (KULN.51)--(KULN.56) gives
\[
 \Pr(G_B)>.9855979264099167608,\qquad
 |\phi_v(Z_B^0)|\le.45\quad\text{on }G_B,
\]
\[
 d_{\nu,B}>.8926485271204980599,\qquad U(X_B^+)\le.87.
 \tag{KUFN.26}
\]
The reference diversity upper bounds are
\(\bar d_\nu\le.404275741942611773\),
\(s_{D,\nu}\le.416459932674500186\). If the actual own measurement
companion satisfies \(|x_u|\ge.85\), positional feature separation
alone gives
\[
 d(Z_B^{\rm actual},Z_u^{\rm actual})
 \ge.5323923214354588021.
 \tag{KUFN.27}
\]
Its proof uses the identical coupled seed position and original
radius-two positional squash. For unchanged resident positions,
\[
 \Pr(|X_A^+|<.85)
 \le Q(((m_A)_1-.85)/\tau)<2\cdot10^{-13}.
 \tag{KUFN.28}
\]
The actual bad-position fraction has expectation at most this
quantity plus \(3.5/N\). Put
\(\mathcal A_{\rm state}=G_B\cap\{d_{N,B}\ge.89,\ M\ge N/2\}\).
This event depends only on the full first-update information,
before the second measurements; \(G_B\cap\{E_N\le10^{-5}\}\)
is contained in it. Condition on that premeasurement information.
The original source measurement kernel is then fixed, and its
bad-partner probability on \(\mathcal A_{\rm state}\) is at most
four times the bad-position fraction. Dropping the smaller
\(E_N\)-good restriction first therefore proves
\[
 \Pr(G_B,E_N\le10^{-5},\text{own mark bad})
 \le\Pr(\mathcal A_{\rm state},\text{own mark bad})
 \le
 \frac4N\mathbb E(\text{bad-position count})
 <8\cdot10^{-13}+14/N.
 \tag{KUFN.29}
\]
This own mark is drawn once and shared by every incoming edge.
It need not be independent of the actual register event; a union
bound subtracts their failure probabilities.

On the good event, the actual fitness has the explicit bound
\[
 f_B\ge G\!\left(
 \frac{1.24356364743449-.87-10^{-5}}
      {.22556648573063+10^{-5}}\right)
 G\!\left(
 \frac{.5323923214354588021-.404275741942611773-10^{-5}}
      {.416459932674500186+10^{-5}}\right)>2.2,
 \tag{KUFN.30}
\]
where \(G(z)=1.1+\tanh(z/2)\). The numerators are positive,
justifying the upper standard deviation bounds. Consequently
the good actual source-and-own-mark event has probability
greater than .9855 for every \(N\ge10^{50}\), in both viscous
normalizations.

Condition on the entire realized second measured array. The exact
signed-source certificate uses the proved actual mean-fitness
bound \(M^{-1}\sum_{i:a_i=1}f_i\le1.614\), source fitness at
least 2.2, degree at least .89, and \(M\ge1000\), to give
\[
 \mathbb E(K_B^{\rm prep}\mid\text{complete measured array})
 \ge1+\frac{2.2(.89)-1.614(1000/999)/.89}{2.2+10^{-6}}
 >1.0648.
 \tag{KUFN.31}
\]
Mandatory dead-row revival has its favorable sign; reverse loss
is subtracted. Its one actual source fitness enters every incoming
gate. Give the other first seed children another passive sublabel;
this persistent-seed sublineage is contained in the complete
initial-seed lineage.

During the second complete update, stored velocities have norm
below two and every actual component-Haar collision velocity has
norm at most four. Both first-kick matrices are stochastic, so
\(|U_i|\le4\). The unrestricted original-noise certificate
(KULN.58)--(KULN.61), for \(|x_B|\le r_*\), gives
landing in \(B=[-.5,.5]^3\) with probability above .9965235
on acceptance and above \(1-3.9\cdot10^{-15}\) on persistence.
Erosion by \(4b\) removes the shared graph/Haar variables from
the sufficient own-noise event. Every counted landing is alive.
Conditional linearity therefore yields
\[
 \mathbb E K_{B,2}^{\rm basin}
 >.9855(.9965)(1.0648)>1.0456
 \qquad(N\ge10^{50}).
 \tag{KUFN.32}
\]
The count is set to zero after actual first extinction, so this
expectation is for the raw killed two-update producer, with
its extinction outcomes retained.
This is a finite-population two-complete-update growth inequality
from the exact native single-seed input, retaining source selection,
both actual measured normalizers, shared Haar collision, both
viscous fields, original noise, native force, cap and marking.

## 7. Retained source-quality interface

The inequality counts second basin descendants. Their next joint
output must still be priced through (KULN.28), including native
reward, diversity, velocity, source degree and exit budgets.
Basin landing alone does not supply the positive fitness/degree
class in (KUFN.31). In particular accepted-jitter offspring can
have potential above the resident mean 1.24356364743449.
Equation (KUFN.32) is therefore a completed finite producer,
with its next source-quality obligation explicitly retained,
rather than a homogeneous branching or class-preservation theorem.
