(sec-nfb-query)=
## 4. Derived stationary smooth query fields without independent input rows

:::{prf:lemma} Uniform smooth query control from the actual marked stationary variance
:label: lem-nfb-query-sup

Use the proved original stationary variance coefficient $C_{\rm stat}$
of (NC.16). Let $q\in[-R,R]^m$, $R>0$, and consider provider
functions $f_q(z)$ on the full marked state. Suppose each mixed query
derivative indexed by $\alpha\in\{0,1\}^m$ is an actual bounded
$L_\alpha$-Lipschitz provider in $d_\omega$. Set

$$
F_N(q)=\mu_N f_q-E\mu_N f_q,\qquad
C_{\rm query}=2^mC_{\rm stat}
                 \sum_{\alpha\in\{0,1\}^m}(2R)^{2|\alpha|}L_\alpha^2.
\tag{NFB.7}
$$

$$
E\sup_{q\in[-R,R]^m}|F_N(q)|^2\le C_{\rm query}/N.
\tag{NFB.8}
$$
The Doob comparison adds its exact bounded-record TV defect multiplied
by the squared bound of this supremum. Every instance below derives
its $L_\alpha$ from the original feature/kernel parameters.
No independent stationary rows or sub-Gaussian stationary concentration
is presumed in this lemma.
:::

:::{prf:proof}
For a differentiable function on $[-R,R]$, the fundamental theorem of
calculus and averaging its starting point give

$$
|u(x)|\le(2R)^{-1}\int_{-R}^R|u(t)|dt+
                                  \int_{-R}^R|u'(t)|dt.
$$
Apply this in each of the $m$ query coordinates. Its tensor expansion
has one term per $\alpha\in\{0,1\}^m$, with coefficient
$(2R)^{-m+|\alpha|}$ times the integral of $|\partial^\alpha u|$.
Cauchy--Schwarz for that integral and then the $2^m$ terms gives

$$
\sup_q|u(q)|^2\le
 2^m\sum_{\alpha\in\{0,1\}^m}
       (2R)^{-m+2|\alpha|}
                     \int_{[-R,R]^m}|\partial^\alpha u(q)|^2dq.
$$
Take $u=F_N$. Each integrand has variance at most
$C_{\rm stat}L_\alpha^2/N$ by the already proved full marked
stationary variance, with its TRUE lower-alive contributions included.
The query volume cancels its displayed negative power of $2R$ and
proves (NFB.8). Bounded derivatives justify differentiation under the
original empirical expectation; approximation covers mixed continuously
differentiable query fields. Finally the supremum is a bounded
measurable full-swarm function, so the existing exact Doob TV comparison
gives its asserted additional cost.
:::
